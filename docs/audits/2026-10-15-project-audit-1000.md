## Scoring method

Each of the original ten phases receives 100 points: required behavior/coverage 30, correctness and access/data integrity 25, failure/recovery behavior 20, operational acceptance 15, reproducible evidence/documentation 10. Operational acceptance allows up to five points for executable preparation and up to ten for observed acceptance in the required environment. Phase 1 uses its actual coverage gate as its acceptance environment; an implementation claim or a matching command name is insufficient.

Partial credit reflects working subsets. An unresolved required behavior or an integrity/recovery blocker keeps a phase incomplete regardless of its numerical score. No current live-VPS/model/payment/Discord result is inferred from previous benchmark notes.

### Why each phase loses points

1. Inventory: hashed source discovery and the checker work, but none of the 179 map rows is marked verified; 114 require decisions and the gate fails. Component/job/dynamic behavior still needs human tracing.
2. Foundation: pinned JavaScript builds and real PostgreSQL integration pass. Current allocations violate the CPU envelope, migration headroom exceeds the memory ceiling, and clean Linux/native-model execution is unproven.
3. Runtime: Himiko, SilverWolf and Cipher use the shared adapter. Other gateways remain separate; deadline replies do not fence all ongoing work or preserve admission limits until real completion.
4. Website: themed public/dashboard routes and core browser flows work. Checkout recovery, review authorization, verification throttling and live OAuth/ingress acceptance remain incomplete.
5. Protection: durable intent and permission enforcement exist. Confirmed expiry bugs can abandon a temporary role, remove a newer ban, or starve later work.
6. Economy: transactional receipts, reservations, concurrency and guild isolation have strong PostgreSQL tests. Listing cancellation is unreachable through the shipped command surface; catalog/legacy reconciliation and live delivery remain open.
7. Commerce: manual UPI/USDT claims and transactional settlement are implemented. Required Razorpay checkout, buyer history, robust recovery and some notification/payout paths are missing or incomplete.
8. AI/voice: local translation/TTS, bounded queues, memory, persona and music recovery exist. Search is a placeholder, stalled inference remains ready, some replies violate the response contract, and model compatibility/quality is unmeasured on the VPS.
9. Operations: immutable manifests, checksummed backups, rollback and watchdog logic have executable tests. A custom-roster generator crash is reproduced; actual off-host/PITR recovery and complete model/persona/reference recovery remain unproven.
10. Launch: procedures and local validation exist, but no fresh target-image startup, live acceptance, workload benchmark, 72-hour soak or authorized cutover evidence was produced.

### Policies and external acceptance still unresolved

- Public checkout accepts a supplied Discord buyer ID. That does not establish caller identity; decide explicit guest gifting versus authenticated ownership and implement the policy consistently. It is not evidence of a staff-settlement bypass.
- Referral rewards are evaluated against the settlement month. Confirm the intended month-boundary behavior and implement tests for checkout before and settlement after the month changes.
- Claims return `notificationStatus: not-configured`; staff notification and reward payout/reconciliation need concrete operator flows.
- Older archives contain card-game/loot and master-management commands excluded by the new registered command list. Reconcile retained behavior inside approved flows or record an explicit product decision; do not automatically reinstate every archived command name.
- Qwen/Hy-MT2 CPU-only images and pinned digests do not establish compatibility with the recorded no-AVX/no-SSE4.2 VPS. Exact images, local model assets, quality, inference timing and simultaneous workload remain unmeasured.
- Live OAuth, Discord effects/audio/reconnect, payment sandbox reconciliation, Linux builds/startup, SSH/ingress, alerts, reboot, off-host recovery and 72-hour soak are not verified in this session. Docker is unavailable in the local audit environment.
